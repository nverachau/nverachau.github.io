N Vera Chau

Visiting Assistant Professor of Finance
Cornell SC Johnson College of Business

My research fields are corporate finance and household finance.

I study technological change and economic growth, particularly the role of financial frictions in shaping technology adoption and investment. In household finance, I focus on the role of intermediaries.

My interests span the broader economy but I have a particular interest in applications to energy and mortgage markets. My methodological interests include structural estimation and neural networks.
